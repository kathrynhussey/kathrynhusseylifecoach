What is coaching?

Coaching is a confidential, collaborative conversation focused on your goals, choices and next steps. Kathryn supports your thinking through attentive listening, useful questions and reflection. You remain the expert in your own life.

How is coaching different from therapy or mentoring?

Coaching usually focuses on where you are now, where you would like to go and what may help you move forward. It is not therapy, medical care or crisis support, and Kathryn does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions. Mentoring often offers advice from the mentor’s own experience, while coaching helps you develop your own answers.

What can I bring to a coaching session?

You might bring a decision, a transition, a goal, a recurring challenge or simply the feeling that something needs to change. You do not need to arrive with a perfectly formed objective. Finding the real focus can be part of the conversation.

What happens in a discovery call?

The complimentary call is an opportunity to meet Kathryn, talk briefly about what you are looking for and ask questions. You can then decide whether the approach and relationship feel like a good fit, with no obligation to continue.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your goals and circumstances. Some people value one focused conversation; others benefit from a series of sessions that allows time for reflection, action and review. Kathryn can discuss the most suitable option during the discovery call.

Are sessions confidential?

Coaching conversations are treated as confidential, subject to the boundaries and exceptions explained in Kathryn’s coaching agreement. She will discuss those boundaries with you before coaching begins.

Where do coaching sessions take place?

Coaching is available online with flexible scheduling. Other available arrangements can be discussed directly with Kathryn.

How do I get started?

Email Kathryn or book a complimentary discovery call. Share only what you are comfortable sharing at this stage, and Kathryn will respond with the next steps.

Why invest in coaching rather than going it alone?

Coaching gives you protected time, an external perspective and a structured partnership focused on your progress. The value can include clearer priorities, thoughtful challenge, accountability and deliberate action. It is not a quick fix: the quality of the outcome depends on the working relationship and your willingness to reflect and act.
